# Plink Break

A little menu bar app for plinking at your Mac's desktop. Pick a popgun, take
aim, and shoot holes in your desktop, or step up to a carnival shooting
gallery of bullseyes, tin cans, and balloons. All pixel art, nothing violent,
and it never touches your real windows.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/geoffalday/plink-break-releases/releases/latest)**,
open the DMG, and drag Plink Break to Applications. It's signed and notarized
by Apple.

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. No special permissions
needed.

## Playing

Plink Break lives in your menu bar: click the target icon there, then
**Start Shooting** (or press ⌃⌥⌘P).

| | |
|---|---|
| Fire | Click or Space |
| Switch weapon | 1, 2, 3 (pistol, machine gun, shotgun) |
| Switch mode | G (Desktop or Gallery) |
| Aim (Gallery) | Mouse or arrow keys |
| Stop shooting | Esc |

**Desktop** puts holes wherever you click. **Gallery** gives you a popgun at
the bottom of the screen and targets to knock down. Holes disappear when you
stop shooting.
